How a county's CVI animal count sets its circle's area. Logarithmic: shrinks big differences, so small counties stay visible; best when a few counties are far larger than the rest. Square root: a gentler squeeze. Linear: area in direct proportion; the largest get very large. Quantile: a few fixed sizes, each holding about the same number of counties. Natural breaks: a few fixed sizes, split where the counts jump. Set how many with Number of bins. This page opens on Logarithmic.

The number of circle sizes for Quantile or Natural breaks. More sizes show finer differences but make a busier legend; 5 is a good default.

Population context

The current legal limits of every incorporated city and town in Washington, from the WA Department of Revenue (updated quarterly), with each city's 2020 Census population. With Population centers also on, cities are outlined from these limits only.

Every 2020 Census place: the incorporated cities and towns (solid outline, their 2020 limits) and the census-designated places, the named unincorporated communities (dashed). With City limits also on, only the census-designated places are drawn, each cut to the part outside current city limits.

People per square mile of land in each 2020 Census tract, in six fixed classes from under 10 to 5,000 and over, drawn under the data. Not with the PNG's Density (choropleth) view, which fills the counties itself.

Names: places of 10,000 people or more statewide; every city and place in the picked counties.

Export & Preview

Each export is one ZIP file, handed to your browser to save (usually your Downloads folder).

Export Details

A name for the ZIP file and the folder inside it. Leave blank for the automatic name, YYYY-MM-DD_Facilities. If a file of that name exists, your browser adds a number.

Your own header text for the branded PNG, up to 80 characters. Leave blank for the automatic title (for example FACILITY DENSITY BY COUNTY).

Output style

The style of both the preview and the exported PNG. Branded: the full WSDA map with logo, teal header and value-box band. Report: an unbranded 6.5-inch landscape picture with a short legend and a small source caption, made to sit in a Word document.

PNG map style

For the preview and the exported PNG only, not the live map. Density shades each county by its facility count; it works with one facility layer shown and CVI activity off, and is grayed otherwise (the PNG then uses Locations). Locations draws one mark per county for each layer shown, sized by its count.

Density needs exactly one facility layer shown and CVI activity off; the PNG uses Locations.

Colors for the Density PNG's counties: light for fewer facilities, dark for more.

PNG Rendering

The background of the preview and the exported PNG. Default is the report-style WSDA background and needs no network. Satellite, Shaded Relief and Hillshade come from Esri's servers; if one cannot be loaded, Default is used and a notice says so. The live map's basemap does not change the PNG.

The detail of the Satellite, Shaded Relief and Hillshade backgrounds. Standard: fast; fine for screens and reports. High: sharper (tile zoom +2) but slower; for print.

Standard: 200 dpi, as CVI writes it (Branded 2200 x 1700 pixels, Report 1300 x 660). High: 300 dpi (Branded 3300 x 2550, Report 1950 x 990), sharper in print, a larger file.

Name markers in PNG

Pick the facility layers whose names print on the branded PNG; leave all unticked for a clean map. A named layer is drawn as its facilities, not one mark per county, and only a facility alone in its area is named. Many names in a busy area crowd the map: pick the one or two layers that matter. PNG only, not the live map.
