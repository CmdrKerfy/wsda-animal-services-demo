CHANGING THE KINDS OF ANIMALS (EMERGENCY RESPONSE)
==================================================

(The same how-to as changing-kinds.html beside this file, which opens in a browser.)

The Emergency page sorts each positive into a kind of animal (for HPAI: Birds,
Cattle, Other animals) by the words in its Species column. The kinds and their
words are kept in one file, in the dashboards' folder:

    site/emergency/types/hpai.js

Each kind gets its own:
  - mark on the map (circle, square, triangle, ...)
  - tick under "Animals"
  - row in the legend and in the map picture's key
  - color in the Overview's "Columns by: Animal"
  - total in the counts

Changing that one file is the whole change; no other file needs it.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------

  - First keep a copy: in the folder, copy hpai.js and paste it there (it is
    named "hpai copy.js" or "hpai - Copy.js"; the page ignores it). If something
    goes wrong, you can put it back.
  - Open hpai.js in a plain-text editor (not Word: it would change the file):
      Windows:  Notepad  (right-click the file, "Open with", "Notepad")
      Mac:      TextEdit (right-click the file, "Open With", "TextEdit")
  - To find a line in it: Edit, Find (Ctrl+F on Windows, Command-F on a Mac).
  - COPY AND PASTE the lines below. Do not type them.
      TextEdit turns typed quotes into curly ones ( ‘ ’ ), and the page cannot
      read those. (Or first turn it off: Edit, Substitutions, Smart Quotes.)
  - After each change: save hpai.js (File, Save), then reload the Emergency
    page in the browser (F5 on Windows, Command-R on a Mac).
  - On the R drive, a changed file changes the page for everyone, and the next
    version's folder replaces it. Send your change to the platform's maintainer
    so it goes into the next version too.


PART A.  ADDING A WORD
======================

Use this when the line under the value boxes names a species the page does not
know, for example:

    1 positive with a species not listed: Opossum

That positive was counted under the last kind, Other animals.

STEP 1   Add the word to its kind
---------------------------------

Do:
  - In hpai.js, find the end of Other animals' words:

      'deer', 'elk', 'wolf', 'dolphin', 'porpoise'] }

  - Add the new word in quotes, after a comma, so it reads:

      'deer', 'elk', 'wolf', 'dolphin', 'porpoise', 'opossum'] }

  - Save, and reload the page.

Expect:
  - Opossum is no longer named in the "species not listed" line.

Good to know:
  - Write the word in small letters. It matches "Opossum", "OPOSSUM" and
    "Virginia opossum" alike.
  - Other animals counts each species by its own name ("12 goats, 1 opossum").
    Birds and Cattle count by the kind's name ("1.2M birds").


PART B.  WORKED EXAMPLE: SHOW MINK APART FROM OTHER ANIMALS
===========================================================

Mink are counted under Other animals, with the triangle. To give them their own
kind, two edits in hpai.js:

STEP 1   Add the Mink kind
--------------------------

Do:
  - Find the cattle kind, the line that starts:

      { key: 'cattle', label: 'Cattle', words: [

  - Paste this new line right under it (before Other animals):

      { key: 'mink', label: 'Mink', words: ['mink'] },

What the parts mean:
  key      a short code name the page uses behind the scenes: small letters,
           digits and dashes only, each kind's different
  label    the name people see on the tick, the legend and the map key
  words    the species words that make a positive this kind, each in quotes,
           with commas between
  The counts use the label in small letters ("7 mink"). To have them say
  something else, such as "7 animals", add  word: 'animals',  after
  label: 'Mink',  in the line.


STEP 2   Take mink out of Other animals
---------------------------------------

Do:
  - In Other animals' words, take out (with its comma and space):

      'mink', 

  - Delete the quotes, the word, the comma and the space after it.
  - A word may be in one kind only; if it is left in both, the page shows a
    red notice (see PART E).


STEP 3   Check the page
-----------------------

Do:
  - Save, reload the page, and load your positives file.

Expect:
  - "Animals" has four ticks: Birds, Cattle, Mink, Other animals.
  - In the platform's made-up test file ("test-tables/em_rules_species.csv",
    which comes with its tests), these species change like this:

      Species       Before                         After
      ------------  -----------------------------  -----------------------------
      Chicken       Birds          circle          Birds          circle
      Mink          Other animals  triangle        Mink           triangle
      Meat Goat     Other animals  triangle        Other animals  diamond
      Pigeon, pig   Birds and Other animals        Birds and Other animals
                    circle, triangle               circle, diamond
      Opossum       Other animals, not listed      Other animals, not listed
                    triangle                       diamond

  - Other animals moved from the triangle to the diamond, because Mink now comes
    before it (see PART C, "Order matters").


PART C.  THE RULES
==================

Order matters
  The marks go by the kinds' order: circle, square, triangle, diamond, pentagon,
  hexagon. So do the colors, the ticks and the legend. A kind put in the middle
  moves every kind after it to the next mark and color.

The catch-all goes last
  The last kind also takes every species no kind names, and the line under the
  value boxes names them. Keep the broadest kind (Other animals) last.
  A positive with no species at all is the first kind (Birds).

Six kinds are told apart
  A seventh kind or later shares the hexagon and the sixth color. Its legend row
  says "same mark as" the sixth. It keeps its own tick and counts.

A word matches at the start of a word, in any case
    "cow"   matches  "Dairy cow" and "Cows"
    "cat"   does not match  "Bobcat"
    "mink"  also matches  "Minke whale"
  Where two words start at the same place, the longer one wins: "pigeon" (a bird
  word) keeps "Racing pigeon" from being a pig. So to keep Minke whales out of
  Mink, add 'minke whale' to Other animals.

A species naming two kinds is both
  "Cattle, Chicken" shows while either kind is ticked, has both marks side by
  side, its animals are counted in neither kind, and a line under the value boxes
  names it.

What the page refuses (each gives the red notice)
  - the same word in two kinds
  - a kind with no words (only the last kind may have none)


PART D.  THE WORK-PC TABLE CHECK KEEPS ITS OWN COPY
===================================================

The HPAI table check (the small tool that checks the agency's HPAI table on the
work PC, in the tools/work-pc folder) keeps its own copy of the same words, so it
can run without the dashboard. Open its file, check_hpai_table.R, in Notepad or
TextEdit the same way and make the same two changes. Its lines are written a
little differently (double quotes, and c(...) around each list), so copy them
from here:

  - Under the Cattle line, the line that starts:

      Cattle = c(

    paste:

      Mink = c("mink"),

  - In the line that starts  `Other animals` = c(  take out:

      "mink", 

Tell the platform's maintainer you changed both files: the platform's automatic
checks flag it when the two lists differ.


PART E.  WHAT A SLIP LOOKS LIKE
===============================

If the page cannot use hpai.js, it leaves HPAI out and shows a red notice at the
top of the sidebar. The emergency type list (under "Positives and zones") then
has no HPAI, and the page would read your HPAI file as the wrong emergency, so
fix the file before going on.

Mink left in Other animals as well:

    The emergency type file "types/hpai.js" was not used. Emergency type "hpai":
    "kinds[3].words" should be words no earlier kind has ("mink" is in kinds[2],
    "Mink", too).

  How to read it: "kinds[3]" means the 4th kind in the list, because the page
  counts from 0: kinds[0] is Birds, kinds[1] Cattle, kinds[2] Mink, kinds[3]
  Other animals. So the word "mink" is in both Mink and Other animals.

A slip in the writing itself (curly quotes, a missing comma, quote or bracket):

    The emergency type file "types/hpai.js" could not be read: a typing slip in
    it, such as quotes made curly or a missing comma or bracket, or the file is
    missing.

To fix: undo the last change (Edit, Undo), or compare your lines with the ones
above, then save and reload again. Still stuck: put back your copy (delete
hpai.js, then rename the copy to hpai.js), and ask the platform's maintainer.

For a maintainer: the other parts of a type file are explained at the top of
hpai.js, and "Adding an emergency type" in docs/DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md says how to
add a type.
